Privacy Policy

Crossword Offline

Effective date: 14 October 2026

In short: Crossword Offline does not sell your information and has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The puzzles work fully offline and your progress stays on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Only if you choose to use "Create with AI" are the photos or topic you pick sent to our server to build your puzzle.

Who we are

This policy explains how the Crossword Offline mobile game (the "App"), published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Apart from the optional "Create with AI" feature and purchases described below, the App does not send any data to us or to third parties. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • Your game progress: solved levels, stars, saved puzzles in progress and your daily streak
  • Your token balance (tokens are earned by playing or bought in the App and are used for hints and AI puzzle credits; they cannot be exchanged for money or transferred)
  • Your settings: language, music, sound effects, vibration and animations

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the App removes it permanently.

Device checks

To keep the game fair, the App checks on your device whether it appears to be rooted, jailbroken or modified. This check runs entirely on your device; the result is not stored or sent anywhere. The App also reads its own version number to show it in Settings.

In-app purchases

You can buy token packs in the App. Payments are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies; we never see or store your payment card details. We receive only the information needed to deliver what you bought, such as a purchase receipt or order identifier, which is used to add tokens to your balance and to restore purchases.

The App shows no advertising.

Create with AI (optional)

If you choose to use "Create with AI", the photos you select (up to 3) and any topic you type are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and processed by [AI PROVIDER] to generate your puzzle. They are used only to create that puzzle, are not used to identify you, and are deleted within [RETENTION PERIOD]. The finished puzzle is saved on your device. Please do not include photos of people or personal documents. If you never use this feature, nothing is uploaded.

Using it needs an internet connection and spends AI credits (tokens).

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Purchases and "Create with AI" are subject to the parental controls of the App Store or Google Play.

Third-party content

Your rights
